Green-Aware SDN Relay Control

for a 5G-assisted flying ad-hoc network

A rescue team sends voice and video to a ground station through four relay drones. An SDN controller picks the relays from each drone's battery, delay and a carbon factor, and moves the traffic away from a weak drone before it fails.

Decision map: when does each planner move the traffic?

In B, adaptive weights move video off UAV1 at 26% and keep it full; fixed weights wait until UAV1 is removed at 19%. In A, greedy adaptive makes the wrong move at 25%, greedy + guard reduces video too early, and joint + guard keeps both flows full until 19%.

Moving earlier is not automatically better, and it is not proof of saving energy.
